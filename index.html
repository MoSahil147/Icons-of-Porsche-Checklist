THE STORIES BEHIND THE SHAPE

Small details.
Big stories.

A few Porsche facts for your next conversation beside a very good car.

01 ORIGINS

The first Porsche was a roadster

The 356 “No. 1” received its road permit in 1948. Its mid-engine layout was different from the rear-engined production 356 that followed.

02 THE 911
901became911

The 911 started as the 901

The first version was shown as the 901. Before production, it became the 911 after Peugeot objected to its three-digit naming pattern.

03 STUTTGART
♞STUTTGART
BADEN-WÜRTTEMBERG

The horse points to Porsche’s home

The horse at the centre of Porsche’s crest comes from Stuttgart’s city seal, a nod to the city where the company is based.

04 RACING
917▦

The 917 took Porsche’s first Le Mans win

In 1970, Hans Herrmann and Richard Attwood won the 24 Hours of Le Mans overall in a 917 KH.

A LOOK UNDER THE REAR DECK

The flat-six.
Six, laid flat.

A 911 signature: six horizontally opposed cylinders, mounted at the rear. Here is the layout in miniature, built from brick-inspired shapes.

Three cylinders sit on each side of the crankcase. Their pistons move in opposition. The 911 places its engine behind the rear axle, giving the flat-six its distinctive position.
